Noto Sans CJK subsets

Designer: Adobe and Google (Noto Sans CJK / Source Han Sans)
Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
Release: Sans2.004
Source paths at that tag:
  Sans/OTF/Japanese/NotoSansCJKjp-Regular.otf
  Sans/OTF/Korean/NotoSansCJKkr-Regular.otf
  Sans/OTF/SimplifiedChinese/NotoSansCJKsc-Regular.otf
  LICENSE (stored as NotoSansCJK-OFL.txt, after the font's copyright line)

scripts/subset-cjk-fonts.py writes NotoSansCJK{jp,kr,sc}-Subset.otf with only
the characters the catalogs use, and records the source and output hashes in
NotoSansCJK-Subset.json. Subsetting drops layout tables and hinting and keeps
the original names, none of which is a Reserved Font Name.
Licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1; see NotoSansCJK-OFL.txt.
