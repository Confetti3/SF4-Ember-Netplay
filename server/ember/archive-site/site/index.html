    Watching a replay

    1. Download a game, or a whole set (.emberpack).
    2. In Ember, open Replays, then Import, and pick the file.
    3. It plays like any of your own replays. USF4 on Steam is needed.

    Every tournament set played on Ember is kept here, and the games of players who share theirs (in Ember: Settings, Training and replays, Share my replays). Classes are each player's class on the rankings today, shown only for players listed there.
