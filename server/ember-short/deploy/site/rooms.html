Joining from this page

  1. You need SF4 Ember Netplay 1.1.0 or newer and an Ember ID. Start Launcher.exe once so your browser knows how to open Ember.
  2. Choose Join in Ember on a room. Your browser may ask first whether to open Ember.
  3. Ember opens Public rooms and asks for a seat. If you are in a game or another room, it waits until you leave.

On Linux or Steam Deck, or if Ember does not open, choose Copy link and paste it in Ember under Online play, Public rooms, Paste room link.

A room on another Ember version can only be joined from that version.
