Joining from this page

  1. You need SF4 Ember Netplay 1.1.0 or newer and an Ember ID. Start Launcher.exe once so your browser knows how to open Ember.
  2. Choose Join in Ember on a room. Your browser may ask first whether to open Ember.
  3. Ember opens Public rooms and asks for a seat. If you are in a game or another room, it waits until you leave.
  4. You need SFV Ember 0.1.0 or newer and an Ember ID. Start SFV Ember once so your browser knows how to open it.
  5. Choose Join in SFV Ember on a room. Your browser may ask first whether to open SFV Ember.
  6. SFV Ember opens Public rooms and asks for a seat. If you are in a game or another room, it waits until you leave.

On Linux or Steam Deck, or if Ember does not open, choose Copy link and paste it in Ember under Online play, Public rooms, Paste room link.

On Linux or Steam Deck, or if SFV Ember does not open, choose Copy link and paste it in SFV Ember under Online play, Public rooms, Paste room link.

Classic rooms have tables with a queue. In an Arcade room everyone has a cabinet and plays whoever has waited longest; King of the hill is the same, and each set's winner stays on.

A room on another Ember version can only be joined from that version.

A room on another SFV Ember version can only be joined from that version.
