Open in Ember

Don't have Ember yet?

  1. You need Ultra Street Fighter IV on Steam, on Windows, or on Linux or Steam Deck through Proton.
  2. Download SF4 Ember Netplay, unzip it anywhere and start Launcher.exe once.
  3. Come back to this page and choose Open in Ember.

Public rooms and tournaments need Ember 1.1.0 or newer.

Then connect Discord

  1. In Ember, choose Ember ID, then Discord, then Connect Discord.
  2. Approve in Discord with the Discord account your tournament site knows.
  3. Go back to the tournament site and register again. It now finds you.

Discord is optional in Ember. Tournament sites that find players by Discord account, such as BluMint, need it.

If Home in your Ember has no Ember ID, your copy is older than 1.1.0.

If Ember does not open

That happens when Ember has not been started on this PC yet, on Linux or Steam Deck, or when the browser blocks it. Copy the link and paste it into Ember instead:

    This page opens Ember from a link, such as a room invitation (embernetplay.link/j#K7QM-4XRT-9PZD), a public room from a bot or site, or a tournament match from a tournament site.

    Ask for the link again, or read about SF4 Ember Netplay.
