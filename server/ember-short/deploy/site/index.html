Ultra Street Fighter IV on Steam

Rollback netplay for Ultra Street Fighter IV

Ember adds rooms with rollback netplay, spectators and training tools to the Steam game. Make a room and send the link, or drop into a public room and play.

Download Ember Browse public rooms

Free and open source. Runs on Windows, and on Linux or Steam Deck through Proton.

What Ember adds

Rooms with rollback

Up to four tables play at once while everyone else watches live. Auto input delay checks each new opponent's connection and picks 1 to 3 frames.

Public rooms

Find players without an invitation. Cards show the host, players and free seats, and Quick join finds an open seat near you. See who is playing

Sets and rotation

Play sets of first to 1 up to first to 10, then Winner stays, Loser stays or Both rotate. The score and win streak sit on the match HUD.

Links that open the game

Copy your room's short link and send it anywhere. Your friend opens it in a browser and Ember joins the room.

Tournaments

Your Ember ID keeps your record. Sites such as BluMint send match links that open the match in Ember, and results are reported for you. Set up tournaments

Made for your setup

Room chat, 14 languages, Steam Input controllers, and Play in the background for streamers who keep OBS in front.

Start playing

  1. Download Ember. setup.exe installs it with a Start menu entry, or unzip the zip into a new folder.
  2. Start Launcher.exe. It finds Ultra Street Fighter IV and starts it with Ember.
  3. Choose Online play. Make a room and send its link, or open Public rooms and join one.

Join a friend's room

  1. Your friend makes a room in Ember and sends you its link.
  2. Open the link and choose Open in Ember.
  3. Or, in Ember, choose Online play, then Join room, and paste the invitation.

Everyone in a room needs the same Ember release.

Public rooms and tournaments

Public rooms are open to anyone with an Ember ID. Browse them here, or in Ember under Online play, then Public rooms.

Tournament sites send match links that open the match in Ember. Connect Discord under Ember ID so the site can find you.

Public rooms and tournaments need Ember 1.1.0 or newer.
