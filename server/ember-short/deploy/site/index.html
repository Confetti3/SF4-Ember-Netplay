EMBERSF4 / Rollback netplay

SF4 Ember Netplay

Ember adds rooms with rollback netplay, spectators and training tools to Ultra Street Fighter IV on Steam. Make a room, send the invitation and play or watch with friends.

Download Ember GitHub Discord

You need Ultra Street Fighter IV on Steam, on Windows, or on Linux or Steam Deck through Proton. Unzip Ember into a new folder and start Launcher.exe.

Join a friend's room

  1. Your friend makes a room in Ember and sends you its link, such as embernetplay.link/j#K7QM-4XRT-9PZD.
  2. Open the link and choose Open in Ember.
  3. Or, in Ember, choose Online play, then Join room, and paste the invitation.

Everyone in a room needs the same Ember release.

Public rooms and tournaments

Public rooms are open to anyone with an Ember ID. Find them in Ember under Online play, then Public rooms, or open a room link that a bot or site shares.

Tournament sites such as BluMint send match links that open the match in Ember. Connect Discord under Ember ID so the site can find you. How to set up tournaments

Public rooms and tournaments need Ember 1.1.0 or newer.
