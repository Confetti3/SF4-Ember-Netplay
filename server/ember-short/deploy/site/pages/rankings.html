Getting on the board

  1. In Ember 1.4.0 or newer, open Online play, Rankings and connect Discord if it asks. Your class belongs to your Discord account, so a new Ember ID keeps it.
  2. In SFV Ember, open Online play, Rankings and connect Discord if it asks. Your league belongs to your Discord account and is kept apart from your class in Ultra Street Fighter IV.
  3. Play public room games that end with a winner. Your classleague appears after a few games against players who have one.
  4. Open Rankings again. That sends the name and flag the board shows for you, the ones rooms show. Open it again after you change them.

To leave the board, choose Stop taking part on the same page. You can change it once a day.

How classes workHow leagues work

Only public room games with a winner count. A class moves only after a clear run of results, and a game against a much stronger or weaker player barely counts. It never drops on its own.

Only SFV Ember's public room games with a winner count, rated apart from Ultra Street Fighter IV. A league moves only after a clear run of results, and a game against a much stronger or weaker player barely counts. It never drops on its own.

Players who have not played for most of a year leave the board until a few games settle their class again. The board here lists up to 5,000 players; when it is full, whoever played longest ago is left off. Ember developers show a Dev mark instead of a class and are not listed.
