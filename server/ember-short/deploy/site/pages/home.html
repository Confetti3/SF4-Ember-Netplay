Ultra Street Fighter IV online, with rollback

Ember adds rollback netplay, rooms, spectating and a training lab to the Steam game. It is free and open source.

Download Ember How to install

For Windows, and for Linux or Steam Deck through Proton. You need your own copy of Ultra Street Fighter IV on Steam.

Rooms for the whole group

Up to four tables play at once while everyone else watches live. Play sets from first to 1 up to first to 10, with the winner or the loser staying on, and keep the score and the streak on the match HUD.

Each player picks their own input delay. Auto checks every new opponent's connection and chooses 1 to 3 frames; you can set 0 to 5 yourself.

Public rooms, open all day

Find a game without an invitation. Quick join takes a free seat near you, in a Classic room with a queue, an Arcade room of sixteen cabinets, or King of the hill.

Games there earn one of Ultra Street Fighter IV's classes, from D up to Grand Master.

A training lab with a frame meter

In Training, Ember draws every move frame by frame: startup, active and recovery, and who is ahead when it ends. Record the dummy, set its position, and wait in Training between games without leaving your room.

Start playing

  1. Install Ember. setup.exe puts it in a folder of its own with a Start menu entry. There is a zip if you would rather not run an installer.
  2. Start Ember. It finds Ultra Street Fighter IV and starts the game with Ember in it. Press F10, or Start at the main menu, to open it.
  3. Choose Online play. Join a public room, or make a room and send its link to your friends.

Joining a friend

Open the link they sent and choose Open in Ember. Or, in Ember, choose Online play, then Join room, and paste the invitation. Everyone in a room needs the same Ember version.

Tournaments

Your Ember ID keeps your record. Sites such as BluMint send match links that open the match in Ember and report the result for you. Set up for tournaments
