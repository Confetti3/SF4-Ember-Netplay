Rollback netplay for Ultra Street Fighter IV

Ember adds rooms with rollback netplay, spectators and training tools to the Steam game. Make a room and send the link, or join a public room and play.

Download Ember How to install

Free and open source. Runs on Windows, and on Linux or Steam Deck through Proton. You need Ultra Street Fighter IV on Steam.

Rooms

Up to four tables play at once while everyone else watches live. Play sets of first to 1 up to first to 10, then Winner stays, Loser stays or Both rotate. The score and win streak sit on the match HUD.

Each player chooses their own input delay. Auto is on by default: it checks each new opponent's connection and picks 1 to 3 frames. You can set 0 to 5 yourself.

Copy a room's short link and send it anywhere. Your friend opens it in a browser and Ember joins the room.

Public rooms

Find players without an invitation. Quick join finds an open seat near you. Classic rooms have tables with a queue, an Arcade room has sixteen cabinets, and in King of the hill the winner stays on.

Games there give you one of Ultra Street Fighter IV's classes, D to Grand Master.

Training lab

Training mode gets a frame meter: startup, active and recovery for each move, and who is ahead when it ends. You can record the dummy, set both positions, and wait in Training between games without leaving your room.

Replays

Ember saves your matches. Watch them in the game, read both players' inputs, or export a video. Shared games go to the replay archive, where anyone can download one and watch it in their own game.

Start playing

  1. Download Ember. setup.exe installs it with a Start menu entry, or unzip the zip into a new folder.
  2. Start Ember. It finds Ultra Street Fighter IV and starts it with Ember. Press F10, or Start at the main menu, to open Ember.
  3. Choose Online play. Make a room and send its link, or open Public rooms and join one.

Join a friend's room

Open the link your friend sent and choose Open in Ember. Or, in Ember, choose Online play, then Join room, and paste the invitation. Everyone in a room needs the same Ember release.

Tournaments

Your Ember ID keeps your record. Sites such as BluMint send match links that open the match in Ember, and results are reported for you. Set up tournaments
