Watching a replay

Replays play in Ember, not in the browser. Download a replay here and put it in Ember's replay folder (in Ember: Replays, then Open the replay folder); it then shows in the Replays list. Or skip the download: Replays, then Replay channel lists the same games in the game and plays them.

What is listed

A game from an Ember room is listed once both players have Discord connected to their Ember ID and neither has turned sharing off. Each entry shows the names, fighters and classes the players had in that game, and the file itself.

To stop sharing your games, open Replays, then Replay channel in Ember and turn Share my room games off. Your replays leave this list at once, and come back if you turn sharing on again.
