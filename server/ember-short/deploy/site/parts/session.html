Connect Discord to open your card

Your points and class belong to the Discord account you connected in Ember. Connect the same account here and the site shows what that account has earned.

  • Your cardFP for every fighter you have played, your BP, frames and the counts behind your awards.
  • Your place on every boardThe BP board and each fighter's FP board show where you stand, and you can open other players' cards.
  • Rating details, shown only to youThe rating, deviation and volatility behind your class, with what each one means and how a game moves them.
Connect Discord Opens Discord to ask for your OK, then brings you back to your card.

Ember has not seen this Discord account

You are signed in, but no Ember player has connected this Discord account, so there is no card to show yet.

  1. In Ember, open Settings, Online and connect this same Discord account.
  2. Play a rated public room game. Your card starts with your first counted game.
  3. Come back and choose Check again.

What Ember reads from Discord

  • Only which account you are, to match the one connected in your game.
  • Not your messages, servers or friends, and nothing is ever posted.
  • Your Discord name and picture are not shown on the site. Cards and boards show the name and flag your game sends.

Without connecting

The class and points boards stay open to everyone. Player cards and your own numbers need a connected account, so the blocks you set in Ember hold here too.
