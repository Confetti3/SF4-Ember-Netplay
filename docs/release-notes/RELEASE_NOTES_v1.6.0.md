# SF4 Ember Netplay v1.6.0

## Changing your Ultra

- **One press from the board.** While you are seated, an Ultra button beside Ready up lists your fighter's Ultras by name. Choose one without opening Table options.
- **After a game.** The post-match prompt shows your Ultra under your answer. Left and Right change it before you choose Rematch or Next game. A game that goes on in place keeps the Ultras it loaded, so with a new Ultra the next game loads first and starts with it.
- **Real Ultra names.** Ready reads, for example, "Ready with Ryu, Metsu Hadoken, against Ken." The fighter page names each Ultra too, not just U1 and U2.
- **Your opponent's pick counts.** Until a player picks, their seat shows their profile's main fighter. A first pick of another fighter now takes back a Ready given against the main, as a later change already did, so the game no longer loads with the wrong Ultra. The table card says what they picked, and the focus moves to your Ultra.
- **Table options are back on the bar** (Y still opens them). Copy room link moved into More….
