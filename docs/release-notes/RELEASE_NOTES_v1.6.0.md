# SF4 Ember Netplay v1.6.0

## Wired players only rooms

- **Wired players only.** A new room setting, off by default, on Create (private or public) and in Room settings. While it is on, players on Wi-Fi can join, watch and chat but cannot take a seat, join a queue or sit at an Arcade cabinet, and the closed seat says why. A connection Ember cannot tell, as on Linux and Steam Deck, can always play. Turning it on moves nobody already seated; a Wi-Fi player still queued is passed over when their turn comes. After plugging in a cable, leave and join the room again. Public rooms show it in the list.
