# SF4 Ember Netplay v1.6.0

## Training controls button

- In offline Training, the button that opens the Training controls on an Xbox-style pad (R3, the right stick click) can now be changed. Open Controllers, pick your pad, Button layout, and use the new Training controls row: select it, then press the button you want.
- It starts on Automatic: R3 opens the controls unless R3 is one of your attacks, in which case the button is off and your attack is left alone. Pick a button or turn it off and your choice always wins; Set Training controls button to automatic goes back.
- F6 and Back with Start always open the controls, whatever this is set to.
- A button chosen here only opens the controls in Training and never attacks there. Outside Training it keeps its attack.
- The on-screen hint shows the button in use, or Back with Start when there is none.
- It cannot share a button with Training record or Training play.

## Public room filters

- **Filter the Public rooms list by region, room type, open seats and connection.** Four rows under the toolbar change with Left and Right: Region (all regions, Near me, or one relay region), Room type (all types, Classic, Arcade or King of the Hill), Open seats only, and Connection (all rooms or only rooms that take wired players alone). If you want Classic sets, set Room type to Classic and the Arcade and King of the Hill rooms are hidden. A room whose type the room service does not say shows only under All types.
- **Your filter is remembered.** It is saved with your settings and is there the next time you open Public rooms. A saved region that Ember no longer offers falls back to all regions.
- **Clear filters.** When the filter hides every open room, the list says so and one press on Clear filters shows them all.
- **Wired only rooms say so.** A room that takes wired players alone shows a Wired only mark on its card.
- **Quick join follows the filter.** It takes the best room the filter lists, near you unless you chose a region yourself.

## Costume and colour grid

- **Costumes and colors are pictures again, all at once.** On a fighter's page, Select on Costume shows a card for every costume you own, each in the color you wear now. Select on a costume shows all of its colors together, and Select on a color saves both. The pad moves across the cards in every direction, and Back steps out without changing anything. Left and Right on the Costume and Color rows still change them in place.
- **From a room, Appearance opens the costume grid straight away,** and picking a color returns to the room.
