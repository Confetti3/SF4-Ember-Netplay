# SF4 Ember Netplay v1.6.0

## Training controls button

- In offline Training, the button that opens the Training controls on an Xbox-style pad (R3, the right stick click) can now be changed. Open Controllers, pick your pad, Button layout, and use the new Training controls row: select it, then press the button you want.
- It starts on Automatic: R3 opens the controls unless R3 is one of your attacks, in which case the button is off and your attack is left alone. Pick a button or turn it off and your choice always wins; Set Training controls button to automatic goes back.
- F6 and Back with Start always open the controls, whatever this is set to.
- A button chosen here only opens the controls in Training and never attacks there. Outside Training it keeps its attack.
- The on-screen hint shows the button in use, or Back with Start when there is none.
- It cannot share a button with Training record or Training play.
