# SF4 Ember Netplay v1.6.0

## Training controls button

- In offline Training, the button that opens the Training controls on an Xbox-style pad (R3, the right stick click) can now be changed. Open Controllers, pick your pad, Button layout, and use the new Training controls row: select it, then press the button you want.
- You can also turn it off with Turn off Training controls button. F6 and Back with Start still open the controls.
- If R3 is an attack on your stick, move this button or turn it off. While it is set, that button only opens the controls in Training and never attacks there. Outside Training it keeps its attack.
- The on-screen hint shows the button you chose, or Back with Start when it is off.
- It cannot share a button with Training record or Training play.
