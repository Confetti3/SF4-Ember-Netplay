# Ember v1.0 release candidate review (2026-09-28 to 09-29)

Branch `review/v1.0` in `sf4-current`, on top of the v1.0 candidate 99e79b7. All changes are
uncommitted in the working tree; the seven new source files are marked intent-to-add.

## How it was reviewed

- Round 1: a Sol (gpt-5.6-sol, high) review plus Claude review agents and a finder workflow
  covering rooms and spectators, the GGPO spectator patch, the Rust helper, the room UI, other
  screens, locales, cross-layer protocol, teardown, code quality and comments. About 70 raw
  findings, merged to 69 (1 P0, 3 P1, 25 P2, 40 P3), each checked against the code before fixing.
  Three were refuted (two-step chat send, dropped control frames, leave reply backlog).
- A v0.9.9 field report ("matches not starting") was diagnosed from the player's logs and fixed.
- Astra (gpt-6-astra, high) then reviewed the fixes for seven rounds. It approved round 7 under
  the owner's bar: block on P0/P1 and on P2s in paths real players hit.
- Every round ran on a fresh `scripts/build-current.ps1` (74/74 ctest at the end) and all 12
  network fixtures (late and stalled spectator, four tables, single table, 16-member relay, Iroh
  room, recovery, authorized match, gameplay, and relay variants).

## Spectators

- A spectator whose stream ended before its battle closed stayed in Playing and silently skipped
  every later match. This was the likely cause of "spectator never rejoins". Fixed.
- A spectator's own stream or setup failure keeps it watching later games but clears its lock-in,
  confirmed by the room and resent until it is, even if the result arrives first. A later Lock in
  by the player wins.
- "Stream lost" and "opponent disconnected" errors no longer vanish when the battle closes.
- The load and start barriers use the roster frozen for that game and reset each game.
- Watchers who pressed Watch mid-game no longer miss the next game when a fighter leaves.
- A spectator off the main menu gets a notice and sits the game out after 5 s.
- A spectator's HUD shows the score its game started with.

## Rooms

- Rematches that stopped starting after 1 to 3 games (v0.9.9 field report): a leftover
  connection from "Check connection" blocked the next game's link. The reused-connection path is
  removed; every game dials a fresh link confirmed by both sides, and failures log their reason.
- "Check connection" results were thrown away when iroh moved the path from IPv4 to IPv6 on the
  same host; a peer's check no longer invalidates yours.
- Rejoining right after a crash no longer loops forever or leaves a ghost seat; a join that is
  never admitted gives up after 8 losses over 45 s.
- Departure notices reach the host, including behind a queue and across a reconnect.
- A room no longer freezes when a voter drops while another leaves; long rooms keep accepting joins.
- Leaving during the start hold no longer cancels the finished game; leaving the queue no longer
  makes you a spectator; delay unlocks when your opponent leaves.

## UI and UX

- Back or Escape at Home hides Ember without leaving the room.
- B on your seat asks first only when you would lose a set score or give the seat to someone
  queued. During a start held for a locked-in spectator, B cancels the start.
- The seat chooser dims choices the room would refuse; dialogs survive a new game; refusals clear
  on screen change or after 5 s; "Cancel stuck game" appears after 600 s.
- Recovery and updater windows show the whole message; "Use keyboard" confirms; the updater can
  start SF4; notices no longer freeze open dialogs; alt-tab clicks are not passed through.
- All player text goes through the 14 catalogs; CJK names and chat draw (+3.3 MB of fonts).
- A 32-bit address-space crash in the font atlas (64 to 128 MB atlases) is fixed: atlases are now
  8 to 32 MB, with a budget test.

## Code quality

- sf4e__NetplayRuntime.cxx (2,143 lines) is split into five files; oversized tests split.
- MenuNavigation uses one modal mode; seat rules live once in the room model.
- History-style and wrong comments rewritten; 121 em/en dashes removed.
- Two flaky tests fixed (one pre-existing).

## Owner decisions made during the review

- Flaky spectator: keeps watching, loses lock-in.
- B leaves in one press unless a score or queued seat would be lost; B cancels a held start.
- Queued players get no Watch row; stuck-game cancel after 600 s.
- Spectator off the main menu sits the game out after 5 s.
- The reused probe connection for gameplay was removed (option 3); a later route change no longer
  withdraws a delay recommendation.
